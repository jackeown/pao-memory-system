Practice stats explained

Counts are per Person, Action, or Object entry: 100 numbers × 3 = 300. New + Learning + Mature = 300.
Due – Practiced entries scheduled for review now. Overlaps Learning and Mature.
New – Entries never graded; seeing the full PAO in feedback doesn't grade the other parts.
Learning – Practiced entries with a review interval shorter than 21 days. Correct first answers return in 1 day; incorrect answers in 10 minutes.
Mature – Practiced entries with a review interval of at least 21 days.
Accuracy (mode) – Correct/total answers in the selected mode across sessions, including retries.
This session – Answers submitted and questions remaining; misses are queued for another try.
